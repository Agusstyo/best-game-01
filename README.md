ORBITFALL — Planetary Defense is a fast arcade shooter where you crew a lone defense satellite locked on a fixed rail around a threatened planet. Meteors and drones rain in across 8 escalating waves; you swing your aim around the orbit, blast them out of the sky before they slam into the surface, chain kills to raise your combo multiplier, and grab dropped power-up cores to stay alive. Clear wave 8 and the planet survives — let its HP hit zero and it falls.

## How to play

- **Mouse** — aim the satellite's cannon
- **Hold Space or the mouse button** — fire
- **P** — pause
- **Enter** — start / restart

## Run it

```sh
python3 -m http.server 8000
# then open http://localhost:8000/game/ in your browser
```
